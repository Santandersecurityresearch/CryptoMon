Analyse a capture

Upload a pcap or pcapng file and see what cryptography the connections in it negotiated. The capture is read in a bounded subprocess.

What happens to your capture. {{ retention }}

A capture records which hosts were contacted and when — including every connection that was happening at the time, not just the one you are looking into. The report keeps the server names it found, so treat a report link the way you would treat the capture.
